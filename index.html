The recommendation

Buy the Baratza Vario W+ at $599.

It is the only grinder in budget that pairs flat burrs with true weight dosing, and Baratza's repairability means it outlasts everything else here. Run the included calibration tool to shift it finer for espresso on day one, then it is exactly the set-the-weight, pull-the-shot experience you want.

If they will stretch $200, the Mignon Libra is the grinder that does the whole job without an asterisk. And if flavor beats workflow for them, the DF64 Gen 2 plus a $40 dosing scale outclasses all three in the cup for about $440 total, which is the move most of the single-dose crowd would actually make.
